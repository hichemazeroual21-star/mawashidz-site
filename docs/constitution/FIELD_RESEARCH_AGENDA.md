## Research program recommendation

**Recommendation:** Run a 3-wave program before listing MVP freeze:

1. **Wave A (Critical):** 15 breeders · 10 buyers · 5 vets · 3 market observation days  
2. **Wave B:** 5–8 intermediaries · connectivity survey n≥100  
3. **Wave C:** Prototype usability on listing + purchase request  

Document results under `docs/constitution/research/` (create when first study completes).  
Mark every product decision that depended on research with a link.
